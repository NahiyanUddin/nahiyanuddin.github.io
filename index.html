Md Nahiyan Uddin

Ph.D. Candidate · Generative AI and Data Systems Researcher

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at The University of Texas at Dallas, advised by Dr. Latifur Khan. My research focuses on reliable retrieval-augmented generation, agentic systems, database intelligence, and scalable data processing. I currently conduct research within the U.S. Department of Transportation-funded Transportation Cybersecurity and Resiliency (TraCR) project.

Email: md.uddin@utdallas.edu

Research Expertise

  • Language Model Systems: Local language models and API-hosted models; agentic workflows with planning, tool use, reflection, and multi-step orchestration.
  • Knowledge-Intensive Reasoning: RAG over large knowledge bases, complex and multi-hop queries, GraphRAG, hierarchical retrieval, dense and sparse search, and reranking.
  • Reliable Generative AI: Fact-aware and citation-grounded generation, hallucination analysis, prompt-injection and data-leakage safeguards, and systematic error analysis.
  • Model and Data Pipelines: Controlled experiments, ablation studies, and scalable ingestion, preprocessing, indexing, validation, and evaluation workflows.
